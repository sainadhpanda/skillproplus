A few helpful details

Questions?
Start here.

Have a question that is specific to your college, project, or timeline? Get in touch with us.

What does SkillproPlus help students with?

We support students with internship learning opportunities, final-year and academic project guidance, and research and technical documentation support.

Can you help me choose a final-year project topic?

Yes. Tell us your course, interests, and any college requirements, and we can discuss project directions and a practical plan with you.

Do you provide IEEE research papers or guarantee publication?

No. We do not claim to have published papers available or guarantee publication. We can discuss responsible research guidance and support with presenting your own work.

How do I ask about an internship or service?

Use our contact page or email skillproplus.edu@outlook.com. Include your area of interest and any relevant deadlines so our team can understand your request.
